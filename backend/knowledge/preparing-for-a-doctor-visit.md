---
title: Getting the most out of a doctor's appointment
url: https://www.ahrq.gov/questions/index.html
condition: general
---
Before the visit: write down your symptoms (when they started, how often, what makes them better or worse), your
questions (most important first) and the list of medicines and supplements you take.

Good questions to ask: What is my diagnosis or what could it be? What are my treatment options, with their
benefits and risks? How long until I should see improvement? What should I do if it gets worse? Which warning
signs mean I should come back sooner or seek urgent care? Are there lifestyle changes (diet, activity, sleep)
that would help in my case?

During the visit: say what matters to you (work, family, budget, food preferences) so the plan fits your life.
Ask the doctor to repeat or write down anything unclear, and bring someone along if that helps.

After the visit: follow the plan, keep notes on how you feel, and book the follow-up the doctor suggested.
