---
title: Using medicines safely
url: https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/
condition: general
---
Never stop, start or change the dose of a prescribed medicine without talking to the doctor or pharmacist who
knows your case: stopping some medicines suddenly can be dangerous.

Keep an up-to-date list of everything you take, including over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, supplements and
herbal remedies, and show it at every appointment: some combinations interact.

Ask a pharmacist when you are unsure how or when to take a medicine, what to do after a missed dose, or whether a
new product is safe with your other medicines. Report side effects to your doctor or pharmacist.

Doses depend on the person (age, weight, kidney and liver function, other medicines): only a clinician who knows
you can set them.
