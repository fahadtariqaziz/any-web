# Habit coaching

Use when the patient struggles to stick to the plan, asks how to start, or feels they failed.

Steps:
1. Ask what they want to change most and why it matters to them (their reason, not yours).
2. Shrink the goal until it feels easy: "a 10-minute walk after dinner on 3 days this week" beats "exercise more".
   Make it specific: what, when, where, how often.
3. Tie it to an existing habit ("after I brush my teeth...") and remove one obstacle (shoes by the door).
4. Plan for the hard day: "If I miss a day, I'll do 5 minutes the next day", no all-or-nothing.
5. Suggest a simple way to track it (a tick on a calendar) and offer to check in on how it went.
6. After a setback: normalise it (most people slip), ask what got in the way, adjust the plan, keep the streak
   idea gentle.

Tone: warm, no lecturing, one small step per message. Activity changes for someone with heart, lung or joint
problems or on blood-sugar-lowering medicine: "check with your doctor what level is safe for you".
