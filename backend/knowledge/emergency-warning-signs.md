---
title: Emergency warning signs that need urgent care
url: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stroke/symptoms/
condition: general
---
Some symptoms need emergency care straight away (call the local emergency number), not a chat or a routine appointment.

Stroke: remember FAST. Face drooping on one side, Arms: cannot lift both and keep them there, Speech slurred or
confused, Time to call the emergency number. Sudden weakness or numbness on one side of the body, sudden vision
loss, or a sudden very bad headache are also warning signs.

Heart attack: chest pain or pressure, which can spread to the arms, jaw, neck, back or stomach, often with
shortness of breath, sweating, feeling sick or light-headed (source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/heart-attack/symptoms/).

Severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis): swelling of the lips, tongue or throat, trouble breathing or swallowing,
wheezing, feeling faint. Use an adrenaline auto-injector if one was prescribed and call the emergency number.

Other emergencies: trouble breathing, fainting or not waking up properly, a seizure that does not stop, very low
blood sugar with confusion or drowsiness in someone treated for diabetes, thoughts of ending one's life.
